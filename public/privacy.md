# Privacy Policy

**Hempon Group**\
**Website:** https://hempongroup.co.ke\
**Effective date:** 28 October 2026\
**Last updated:** 28 October 2026

## 1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Hempon Group ("we", "us", or "our")
collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you visit
our websites, browse our apps catalogue, contact us, request a website
or software project, or use products and services we provide.

A product may have its own privacy notice or agreement with additional
details. Where applicable, read that notice alongside this policy.

## 2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

-   **Contact details:** name, email address, telephone number, business
    name, and details you include in an enquiry.
-   **Project information:** requested services, project requirements,
    budget range, preferred timeline, and other information you choose
    to provide.
-   **Messages:** communications sent by email, WhatsApp, social media,
    contact forms, or other channels.
-   **Technical information:** browser and device information, pages
    visited, and diagnostic or security logs, where collected by our
    hosting or website tools.
-   **Account or service information:** information needed to deliver,
    maintain, secure, or support a product or service you choose to use.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, national identity
numbers, or other sensitive information through a general enquiry form
or social media unless we specifically request it through an appropriate
secure process.

## 3. How we use information

We may use information to:

-   respond to enquiries and communicate with you;
-   understand and scope website, software, design, or consulting
    projects;
-   prepare proposals, estimates, contracts, invoices, and project
    deliverables;
-   provide, operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure our websites
    and services;
-   manage customer relationships and provide support;
-   improve our websites, catalogue, products, and user experience;
-   meet legal obligations, protect our rights, and prevent misuse; and
-   send updates or marketing communications where permitted by law and,
    where required, with your consent.

An enquiry is not permission to send unrelated marketing where consent
or another lawful basis is required.

## 4. Legal basis and data minimisation

We aim to handle personal information for clear and legitimate purposes
and collect only information that is relevant and reasonably necessary.
Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to respond
to your request, take steps towards or perform a contract, comply with a
legal obligation, protect legitimate interests, or rely on your consent.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to legal or
contractual limits and processing already carried out before withdrawal.

## 5. WhatsApp, email, and third-party services

Some forms may open WhatsApp with a message pre-filled for you to review
and send. **The message is not sent until you choose to send it in
WhatsApp.** If you continue, your information will be handled by
WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, infrastructure,
analytics, email, file delivery, customer communication, payment
processing, or product functionality. Those providers may process
information on our behalf or independently under their own terms.

Our websites may link to external websites, applications, social
networks, or downloads. We do not control third-party websites and are
not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies
before sharing information with them.

## 6. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information as a mailing list. We may
disclose relevant information:

-   to service providers who help us operate or deliver a requested
    service;
-   to project collaborators where necessary and appropriate for your
    project;
-   where you ask or authorise us to share it;
-   where required by law, a court, or a competent authority; or
-   where reasonably necessary to protect people, our systems, our
    rights, or the security of our services.

We seek to limit disclosures to what is appropriate for the relevant
purpose.

## 7. International processing

Some providers may store or process information outside Kenya. Where
applicable, we will take steps required by Kenyan data protection law
concerning international transfers, including appropriate safeguards or
another permitted basis.

## 8. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary
for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to you,
delivering a project, maintaining business records, resolving disputes,
and meeting legal obligations.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to
protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or
disclosure. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be
guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019,
you may have the right to:

-   be informed about how your personal information is used;
-   request access to personal information we hold about you;
-   request correction of inaccurate or misleading information;
-   request deletion where legally applicable;
-   object to, or request restriction of, certain processing;
-   withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
-   lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection
    Commissioner in Kenya.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to
verify your identity and clarify the request before acting. Some
information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires
it.

## 10. Children

Our general business websites and services are not intentionally
directed at children. Please contact us if you believe a child has
provided personal information to us inappropriately, and we will
consider appropriate steps.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, or legal
obligations change. We will publish the updated version on the relevant
website and revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes may be
highlighted where appropriate.
